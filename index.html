HOW WE DO THIS

Expert care.
One simple step at a time.

From your first appointment to ongoing support, we make online physiotherapy personal, clear, and easy to follow.

  1. 01

    STEP 01

    Book your virtual appointment

    Complete a short, two-minute form about your symptoms and pain history. Share your preferred time, and our clinic will confirm your appointment.

  2. 02

    STEP 02

    Meet your specialist on video

    Connect one-on-one over a video call. Your physiotherapist assesses your movement, posture, and pain triggers in real time.

  3. 03

    STEP 03

    Understand your pain

    Get a clear explanation of what may be contributing to your pain and how physiotherapy can help, with a plan tailored to your needs.

  4. 04

    STEP 04

    Begin your 45-minute session

    Work one-on-one with your physiotherapist in a live, 45-minute treatment session, with exercises suited to your ability and goals.

  5. 05

    STEP 05

    Get guidance as you move

    Receive real-time feedback on your technique, so you can practise your exercises with the right posture and greater confidence.

  6. 06

    STEP 06

    Stay supported along the way

    Ask questions, review your progress, and adjust your plan with your physiotherapist. Get encouragement to keep moving towards your goals.

F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is online physiotherapy really effective?

    Yes! With proper assessment and real-time guidance, online physiotherapy can be just as effective for many conditions as in-person visits.

  • What equipment do I need?

    A smartphone or laptop with a camera, internet access, and some basic items like a towel, pillow, or exercise band.

  • Is my personal information secure?

    Absolutely. All video calls and health data are handled via secure, HIPAA-compliant platforms.

  • What if I need hands-on therapy?

    If manual therapy is needed, we’ll guide you to the nearest trusted clinic, or teach self-techniques where possible.
