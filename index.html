Meet Dr. Swapnali Bajaj: Your Guide to a Stronger, Pain-Free Life—From Anywhere

Dr. Swapnali Bajaj isn't just a qualified physiotherapist—she's someone who really gets it. With over 10 years of experience treating people with chronic pain, movement issues, sports injuries, and poor posture, she's helped hundreds of individuals regain their strength, confidence, and independence.

She holds a Master's in Orthopedic Physiotherapy (MPT Ortho) and is certified in advanced techniques like Dry Needling and Tele-Rehabilitation. But more than her credentials, what sets her apart is how she treats you.

Whether you're visiting her at the clinic or connecting online from the comfort of your home, Dr. Bajaj takes the time to listen carefully, understand what you're going through, and explain everything in simple, clear language. No complicated medical talk—just real advice that makes sense and works.

F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is online physiotherapy really effective?

    Yes! With proper assessment and real-time guidance, online physiotherapy can be just as effective for many conditions as in-person visits.

  • What equipment do I need?

    A smartphone or laptop with a camera, internet access, and some basic items like a towel, pillow, or exercise band.

  • Is my personal information secure?

    Absolutely. All video calls and health data are handled via secure, HIPAA-compliant platforms.

  • What if I need hands-on therapy?

    If manual therapy is needed, we’ll guide you to the nearest trusted clinic—or teach self-techniques where possible.
